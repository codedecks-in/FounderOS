# Playbook: Capture Post-Release Learning

**Owner:** Product owner with engineering and support contributors  
**Inputs:** Success measures, release evidence, user feedback, incidents, costs  
**Outputs:** Updated assumptions, product decision, prioritized follow-up, FounderOS improvement

## Procedure

1. Schedule review after the measurement window defined before release. Confirm metric definitions, baselines, target cohorts, and data quality.
2. Compare outcomes with hypotheses and targets. Include qualitative user feedback, support burden, reliability, security events, delivery effort, and operating cost.
3. Separate correlation, observed facts, and causal claims. Note small samples, missing data, and external factors.
4. Decide whether to continue, change, expand, or stop the feature/experiment. Record the decision owner and rationale.
5. Create prioritized follow-up items linked to evidence, requirements, incidents, or open risks. Update discovery and specifications through the change playbook.
6. Identify a reusable improvement to a template, playbook, agent, or quality gate. Make the smallest evidence-supported change and review it for consistency.

## Gate

Close the review only when results, limitations, decision, owners, due dates, and follow-up measurement are recorded. No learning is inferred from a metric without validating what it measures.
