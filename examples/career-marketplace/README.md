# Career Marketplace Reference Project

This example applies FounderOS to the tech-career expertise marketplace described in [discovery.md](discovery.md). It is a product and engineering design exercise, not evidence that market assumptions have been validated or that a production system exists.

## Artifact sequence

1. [Founder decision register](decision-register.md) — selected baseline and unresolved choices
2. [Market research](market-research.md) — evidence gaps and validation plan
3. [PRD](prd.md) — MVP behavior and acceptance criteria
4. [Architecture](architecture.md) — system boundaries and undecided choices
5. [Domain model](domain-model.md) — core marketplace concepts and rules
6. [Database design](database-design.md) — logical data model
7. [API specification](api-specification.md) — candidate contracts
8. [Security architecture](security-architecture.md) — assets, threats, and controls
9. [UI/UX specification](ui-ux-specification.md) — pages and key states
10. [AI feature decision](ai-feature-design.md) — records why AI is excluded from MVP
11. [Implementation plan](implementation-plan.md) — sequenced increments
12. [Test strategy](test-strategy.md) — requirement validation approach
13. [Release plan](release-plan.md) — readiness, rollout, and recovery gates

All artifacts are drafts. Open questions in `discovery.md` remain unresolved unless an artifact explicitly records a proposed MVP assumption. No vendor, policy, market claim, or legal conclusion is treated as approved without evidence and founder review.
